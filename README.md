### 📡 RFMS & Network Availability

> **RFMS — Remote Fiber Management System**

- 📡 Monitor **Network Elements (NE), links and services** for availability.
- 🚨 Monitor and investigate **network alarms**.
- 🔍 Identify **NE / Link / Service-down conditions** that may affect service availability.
- 🎫 Raise and coordinate **tickets/incidents** for service-impacting issues.
- 🔧 Perform initial investigation and troubleshooting.
- 🤝 Coordinate with concerned teams for **issue resolution and service restoration**.
- 📊 Track incidents until resolution to support **network and service availability**.
- ⚠️ Monitor critical and recurring alarms and escalate according to operational procedures.

---

### 🔥 Firewall Log Monitoring

- 🔥 Extract and review **Firewall Deny and Timeout logs**.
- 🔍 Use relevant firewall logs as supporting information during **connectivity and service troubleshooting**.
- 📊 Review log information to help identify possible **traffic/connectivity issues**.
- 🤝 Coordinate with concerned teams when firewall-related investigation is required.

---

### 🖥️ IT & Application Support

- 🖥️ Provide technical support for **IT systems, applications and infrastructure**.
- 🐧 Support **Linux-based applications and systems**.
- ⚙️ Perform application **start/stop and basic service management**.
- 📊 Support **dashboard and monitoring applications**.
- 📦 Assist with application builds, deployment-related activities, backups and file management.
- 🔍 Perform file verification, comparison and troubleshooting.
- 📝 Follow defined IT support and operational procedures.
- 🤝 Coordinate with internal teams for **issue resolution, escalation and follow-up**.
